# RotMG Meta Research — 2025+ "Street Smarts"

> Companion to `CONTEXT.md`. This is the **grind/meta intel layer** (how real players progress),
> distinct from the wiki "book smarts" the app already scrapes. Every claim here is sourced from
> 2025-current resources (RealmEye wiki + official DECA patch notes). Mark anything inferred as ⚠️.
> Goal: drive updates to `dungeons.json`, `pot-routing.json`, and new `biomes.json` / exalt data.

## 0. TL;DR — what changed since our data was curated
- **The Realm Rework is live.** The realm is now divided into **biomes** (Rookie → Adept → Veteran),
  each with a **Beacon** guarded by a **Beacon Guardian**. This is the single biggest gap in our app —
  we have **zero** biome/beacon coverage. (Listed as the #1 unbuilt feature in CONTEXT.)
- **Realm closing changed.** No more "kill all Ents/Liches." A **Realm Score** (every kill contributes)
  fills to 100%, then Oryx closes the realm. Implication: there's no "close the realm" checklist to model;
  progression is biome traversal + Realm Score.
- **Stat pots now come from biomes, not just dungeons.** Adept-biome Godlands-tier enemies drop the 6 stat
  pots; **Veteran biomes drop the once-dungeon-exclusive Life & Mana pots.** → Our `pot-routing.json`
  assumption that Life/Mana are endgame-dungeon-only is **outdated**.
- **Modern dungeons missing from `dungeons.json`:** Crystal Cavern, Kogbold Steamworks, Spectral
  Penitentiary, Moonlight Village, Parasite Chambers, Sulfurous Wetlands, Crawling Depths, Ancient Ruins,
  Ice Citadel/Ice Tomb, Deadwater Docks (+ The Void as distinct from Cultist Hideout).
- **The real endgame grind is Exaltations** (permanent per-class +stats), not just 8/8. We model none of it.
- **Forge/crafting** changes "what to grind": you grind UTs to dismantle for mats + target-craft via blueprints.
- **Account Levels** permanently raise starting gear tier → the early grind is shorter than our data assumes.

## 1. The new Realm: biomes, beacons, beacon guardians  (answers "which biome/beacons to go to")

**Flow:** Rookie biomes (leveling) → Adept biomes (the 6 stat pots) → Veteran biomes (Life/Mana pots +
Wine Cellar gear). Biomes are connected by **roads** (speed boost). Each biome has a **Beacon**: inactive
beacons are guarded by a **Beacon Guardian** you must kill to activate. Activated beacons = **fast-travel
on the minimap**, a **safe heal zone** (enemies can't enter), and road connections.

**Beacon Guardians = Heroes of Oryx.** Killing one: (a) can spawn an **Encounter** (event boss), and
(b) has a **rare chance to drop that biome's UT**. So guardians are both progression and a UT-farm target.

### Adept biomes (Godlands-tier — primary stat-pot farming)
| Biome | Stat pots (biome enemies) | Dungeon portals that spawn here |
|---|---|---|
| Coral Reefs | (Fishing hub, no guardian) | — |
| Sprite Forest | **Dex, Speed, Vit** | Sprite World, Magic Woods |
| Haunted Hallows | **Wis** | Undead Lair, Mad Lab, Cursed Library |
| Shipwreck Cove | **Speed, Dex** | Ancient Ruins, Cave of a Thousand Treasures |
| Dead Church | **Att, Wis** | Haunted Cemetery |
| Risen Hell | **Vit, Def** | Manor of the Immortals, Abyss of Demons, Cursed Library |
| Abandoned City | (mixed) | Ancient Ruins, Mad Lab, Toxic Sewers |

### Veteran biomes (tougher — add Life/Mana pots + Wine Cellar gear tier)
| Biome | Stat pots (biome enemies) | Dungeon portals that spawn here |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Sea Abyss | **Att, Vit, Mana** | Ocean Trench, Cnidarian Reef, Davy Jones' Locker |
| Carboniferous | **Vit, Wis, Mana** | Woodland Labyrinth, Crawling Depths |
| Floral Escape | **Speed, Vit, Life** | Lair of Draconis, Mountain Temple, Sulfurous Wetlands |
| Sanguine Forest | **Att, Wis, Life** | Tomb of the Ancients, Lair of Shaitan, Parasite Chambers |
| Runic Tundra | **Vit, Def, Wis, Mana** | Kogbold Steamworks, Ocean Trench, Ice Citadel |

### Seasonal biomes (rotate)
- **Eternal Frost** (Oryxmas): Ice Tomb, Ice Cave, High Tech Terror
- **Relentless Springs** (Easter): Queen Bunny Chamber, **Crystal Cavern**, Moonlight Village

> ⚠️ Beacon-guardian → biome names are confirmed (e.g., Sprite Forest = "Legion Fey Guardian",
> Veteran guardians = "Legion Colonel/Commissioner/Missionary/Principal"); the exact per-biome UT each
> guardian drops still needs a per-page pass when we build `biomes.json`.

**App implication:** add `biomes.json` keyed by biome → `{tier, beaconGuardian, statPots[], dungeonPortals[], ut?, seasonal?}`.
For a stat goal, the app can now recommend a **biome to farm** (fast, repeatable) *and* the dungeons that spawn there.

## 2. Modern stat-pot farming  (updates `pot-routing.json`)

Two-track model now:
1. **Biome farming** — kill the matching Adept biome's Godlands-tier enemies for the 6 stat pots; Veteran
   biomes for Life/Mana. Lower risk, no boss gate, scales with realm uptime.
2. **Dungeon farming** — bosses with ~100% soulbound pot drops (still the reliable single-stat sources).

**Greater-potion dungeons (the upgrade target — each Greater = bigger jump than a normal pot):**
- **Kogbold Steamworks** — Greater pots of all **6 stats** (miniboss ~1, boss up to 3; **Advanced** = +2 more).
  No Life/Mana. Often the single most pot-efficient farm.
- **Fungal Cavern** & **Crystal Cavern** — Greater pots of all 6 stats **+ Life pots** + heavy white-bag UTs.
- **Lost Halls** — Def, Att, Life, Mana at high rates.

**Curated early single-stat sources still valid** (Sprite World→Dex, Snake Pit→Spd, Undead Lair→Wis,
Abyss→Vit, Toxic Sewers→Def, Puppet Master's Theatre→Att) — but now framed as "or just farm the parent
Adept biome." **Correction to our data:** Life/Mana are **no longer endgame-only**; Veteran biomes +
Kogbold-adjacent content make them mid-game accessible.

> Note from patch notes: leveling was sped up and Spd/Dex/Def stat-per-level growth increased → **fewer
> pots needed overall** than our `potsToMax` math may assume. Re-verify pots-to-max against current class
> max tables (the refresh scraper already pulls live class maxes, so this is partly self-correcting).

## 3. Exaltations — the real endgame grind  (answers "what's worth grinding / when to stop")

Completing an exalt dungeon **on an 8/8 character** banks progress. **5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25** completions of a
given dungeon = **+1/+2/+3/+4/+5** to its stat(s) (**+5 each tier for Life/Mana, max +25**), **permanent &
per-class** — every future character of that class inherits it. This is THE post-8/8 loop.

### Dungeon → exalt stat (verified per-boss; ⚠ = secondary-stat grouping inferred)
| Dungeon | Boss | Exalts |
|---|---|---|
| The Nest | Killer Bee Queen | **Dexterity** |
| Kogbold Steamworks | Factory Control Core | **Dexterity** |
| Fungal Cavern | Crystal Worm Mother | **Vitality** |
| Crystal Cavern | Crystal Entity | **Vitality** |
| Ice Cave | Esben the Neurotic | **Speed** (⚠ +Wisdom) |
| Cultist Hideout | Malus | **Speed** (⚠ +Wisdom) |
| The Void | Void Entity | **Mana** |
| Lost Halls | Marble Colossus | **Defense** |
| Spectral Penitentiary | Soulwarden Murcian | **Attack** |
| The Shatters | Twilight Archmage (2nd) / Forgotten King | **Attack**; **Life+Mana** (⚠ +Defense) |
| Oryx's Sanctuary (O3) | Oryx 3 | **Life** (⚠ +Defense) |
| Moonlight Village | last Dancer (Genji/Miko/Kaguya) | **Life / Attack / Mana** (by last dancer) |

> ⚠ RealmEye groups Defense with the Life bosses and Wisdom with the Speed bosses, so several dungeons
> likely grant a 2nd stat. Pull the live exaltation table when building `exaltation.json` to confirm pairings.

### Efficient vs. waste-of-time (community consensus)
- **Best fame/exalt-per-hour:** **The Nest** (fast, Advanced = +90% XP), **Ice Cave**, **Kogbold** — quick,
  repeatable, safe-ish. Grind these for exalts.
- **Avoid for pure farming:** **The Shatters** and **O3** are very time-consuming/dangerous relative to
  reward — do them for completions/loot/the specific exalt, not as a fame/exalt grind.
- **Don't run a hard dungeon for a stat you could get cheaply** (the classic "don't run Shatters for Def at
  0/8"). Match dungeon to your current maxed count.

## 4. When to STOP grinding something  (the "waste of time" signal the app should surface)

- **Hard stat cap on ATT/DEX:** gains are **halved past 75** ATT/DEX (diminishing returns) — flag when a
  build is pushing past it via gear/exalts.
- **A stat is maxed (8/8 for that stat):** stop all pot farming for it (app already does this per-stat).
- **You out-tier a pot dungeon:** once you can clear **Greater-pot dungeons** (Kogbold/Fungal/Crystal) or
  farm the matching **biome**, single-pot starter dungeons (Sprite World, Snake Pit…) become inefficient →
  app should *demote* them once the player is ready for greaters.
- **Character hits 8/8:** low/mid dungeons stop giving stat progress → they're now only fame/UT. The app
  should switch that character's recommendations to **exalt dungeons + greater-pot/biome farming**.
- **You already have the UT / can forge it:** stop RNG-grinding a drop you can target-craft (see §5).

**App implication:** add a per-dungeon `obsoleteWhen` signal (e.g., `maxedCount >= X`, or "player has a
greater-pot dungeon unlocked") so the goal list can gray-out / drop "waste of time" dungeons.

## 5. What items are worth grinding  (answers "what items to chase")

Our app already ranks gear by RealmEye Power Level / score — keep that. The **street-smarts additions**:

- **Forge / Blueprints (changes grind from RNG → targeted):** Only **UT/ST** items yield forge materials
  when dismantled (tiered gear yields nothing). ~**4 UTs → 1 craft** of the same bracket. **Blueprints**
  permanently unlock a UT for crafting; a duplicate blueprint slotted during forging cuts Forgefire cost.
  Forgefire = **500/day, cap 2000** (as of May 2025). → The app can flag a chased UT as **"forgeable"**
  (grind feeder UTs + blueprint) vs **"drop-only"**.
- **Account Levels:** permanently raise the **starting gear tier** on every new character → de-prioritize
  grinding low tiered gear; the floor is higher now.
- **Shiny hunting:** cosmetic ultra-rare item variants — pure long-tail chase, not progression. The app can
  acknowledge but should not treat as a goal.
- **Wine Cellar tier:** new equipment tier from Veteran biomes — a mid/late gear target worth surfacing.

## 6. Concrete app data-model changes this research implies
1. **New `biomes.json`** — biome → tier, beacon guardian, stat pots, dungeon portals, UT, seasonal flag (§1).
2. **`dungeons.json` additions** — add missing modern dungeons; tag each with `biome`, `biomeTier`
   (rookie/adept/veteran), `greaterPots: bool`, `exaltStats: []`, and an `obsoleteWhen` rule (§3, §4).
3. **`pot-routing.json` rewrite** — two-track (biome + dungeon); move Life/Mana to mid-game via Veteran
   biomes; add greater-pot dungeons as the late upgrade (§2).
4. **New `exaltation.json`** — dungeon → exalt stats + 5/10/15/20/25 milestones; flag efficient vs.
   waste-of-time for fame/exalt grinding (§3).
5. **Gear goals** — add `forgeable`/blueprint awareness; surface Wine Cellar tier (§5).
6. **Refresh scraper** — add biome pages + exaltation page to the token-free weekly pipeline so all of the
   above stays auto-patchable (consistent with the project's "never hand-guess, always source" rule).

These six feed directly into the planned **overlay HUD** (target biome/beacon + top exalt dungeon + next stat
pot source for the active character).
