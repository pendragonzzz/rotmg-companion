Corruption Tether

This transmogrified crook bolsters the ability to peer into pocket dimensions and cling to the essence within. Such a characteristic arguably makes the staff itself sentient, a harrowing trait for the tool which gave the Shattered Kingdom its name.

Notes
Part of the Twilight Archmage Set.

This staff is one in a few that fire only one shot, able to beat the T14 Staff of the Fundamental Core and even Superior starting at 49/44 DEF (before any group buffs). As this staff also has a dampened range, it is quite similar to a spellblade in its usage and function, though unlike spellblades, the Tether does not pierce.

This staff always beats the T13 Spellblade of the Heavens Light and T12 Staff of the Cosmic Whole in terms of damage, though always loses out to the T14 Spellblade of the Everlasting Death.

Before Exalt Version 5.0.0.0 (July 2024), this item did not drop from Avatar of the Forgotten King, had a dismantling value of 80 .

Before Exalt Version 5.15.0.0 (Aug 2025), this item dealt 150-180 damage.
