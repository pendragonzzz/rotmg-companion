Notes
The Demon Blade stands out among the UT Swords for its signature two-shot, V-shaped firing pattern creating a 25 degree angle. It has a short true range, but if you can hit both shots, it’s one of the strongest weapons in the game. It beats all swords except the Pixie-Enchanted Sword, and Pixie is only stronger against 0 DEF targets.

Bosses that are safe to stand on, such as Dr. Terrible and Pentaract Towers, are obvious targets. Easier bosses like Stheno the Snake Queen can also be safe to DBlade at max DEF.

Certain abilities can make getting close more viable. Knights, a Paladin with the Seal of Blasphemous Prayer or a Warrior using the Helm of the Juggernaut can safely pummel many enemies at close range without much danger.
