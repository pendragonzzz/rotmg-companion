Hivemaster Helm

A nefarious helm combining pheromones and Killer Honey to captivate Killer Bees into servitude.

Tier UT
MP Cost 80
On Equip +6 DEF, +6 SPD
Effect(s) On party: Inflicts Berserk within 4 tiles for 6 seconds
Summon 3 bees to aid the player in combat for 6 seconds
  • Bee Attack: 120 damage every 0.6 seconds
  • Bee Range: 4 sqrs
  • Red Bee Effect: Inflicts Curse for 4 seconds after being active for 1.6 seconds
XP Bonus 7%
Soulbound
Feed Power 1,000
Forging Cost 60 / 160 / 900 / 2 UT(s)
Dismantling Value 15 / 40
Used Dust
Loot Bag
Drops From Killer Bee Queen
The Beekeeper
Honeyed Epic Quest Chest
Obtained Through Reward Ticket
Blueprint
Drops From Killer Bee Queen
Obtained Through The Tinkerer (50x Schematic)

Notes:

A unique helm that gives up its speedy ability and some of its defense to summon 3 bees to help you in battle. It also gives a hefty speed boost to compensate for the loss of Speedy.

The bees orbit you for 6 seconds and inflict 120 damage each every 0.6 seconds each to anything near them by firing short-ranged beams at them (range of 4.5 tiles). These beams are not projectiles, so will never miss. Each bee will perform this damage separately, so at 0 defense this helm will give 600 dps. Note that the bees’ damage does not ignore defense. In addition, after being active for 1.6 seconds the red bee’s next attacks will apply the Curse effect. However, the Curse can only affect up to 3 targets at any given time.

Overall, this helm is useful when DPS is your main priority and speed / survivability are not. These bees can churn immense DPS, along with a curse effect, not to mention that curse increases the bee’s damage output. It can also be desirable in fights like the Killer Bee Queen or Marble Colossus, where the mobility from a tiered helm would be detrimental

History
Before Exalt Version 1.5.0.0 (May 2021), this item had a cooldown of 7.5 seconds, the Red Bee only had a 50% chance of applying Curse, and the bees were not summons.
